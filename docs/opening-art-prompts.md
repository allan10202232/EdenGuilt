# Opening cinematic art prompts

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, using one generation per scene. No fallback CLI/API and no post-generation image editing were used. Assets were copied into this project from the built-in generation output.

## References

- `WechatIMG1395.jpg`: dark crystalline wasteland/mining settlement pixel art style.
- `assets/art/originals/main character shape1.png`: protagonist mask, red horns, red eyes, and cloak identity (discovery only).
- `assets/art/generated/opening/soul_extractor.png`: extractor design reference (discovery only).

## Final assets and prompts

### `assets/art/generated/opening/soul_extractor.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: opening cinematic artwork for a 2D environmental action platformer. Create a NEW illustration, using supplied image only as a visual style reference. Landscape 16:9, ideally 1536x864. Crafted crisp pixel art, limited dark blue-grey and charcoal palette, tiny warm accents, coherent block pixels and stepped silhouettes, fine detailed clusters, dramatic readable scene. Entire composition is artwork edge to edge. NO text, letters, logo, subtitles, UI, decorative border, watermark, montage, or panels. Not 3D, not vector, no soft airbrush or photographic textures. Subject: a single spherical handheld Soul Extractor, centered and large enough to see its design, resting in a shallow depression of cracked dead soil. Its weathered bronze and steel segmented shell has three curved metal ribs around a bright green circular glass life core; small grooves and amber indicator specks, clearly a technological ball and not an anatomical organ. Very faint green tendrils of light drawn up from the ground beneath it, a few white crystalline stones, sparse barren waste. Close view of the object occupies about one third of frame, with dark atmospheric surroundings and green light reflected on broken earth. Ominous stillness. Input image 1: palette and pixel art rendering reference only, do not copy its settlement layout.

## Review

All four assets are opaque landscape pixel-art illustrations with no embedded text. The Eden scene contrasts the forest-and-lake ring with the crystalline desert, the undercity emphasizes miners and poor shelters, and the discovery scene preserves the existing protagonist identity and the extractor's green core and ribbed bronze shell. Narrative captions and reveal effects are rendered separately by Godot.
